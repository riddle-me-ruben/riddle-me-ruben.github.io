# Archived: pre-pivot tab components

These six components were the entire portal before Sprint 1 restructuring. They
are kept here for reference and are **not routed or imported anywhere**.

They were built against the earlier project concept (an AI-assisted academic
advising platform) and against a tab-based layout. Both are now obsolete:

- The selected project is **ShareTab**, a shared-expense tracker. The charter,
  business case, risk register and estimation figures in these files describe a
  different product and must not be reused as-is.
- The portal guidelines require real pages and subpages with their own URLs, not
  tab state, so that individual documents can be linked and reviewed directly.

Some of the *structure* here is still worth borrowing when the real Sprint 1 and
Sprint 2 documents get written — the RACI grid, the power/interest matrix, the
RAID log layout and the COCOMO / story-point tables are all reusable shapes.
Copy the layout, not the content.

Delete this folder once nothing in it is needed.
